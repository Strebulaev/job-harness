title: "Telegram bot chats are not end-to-end encrypted"
status: active
date: 2026-10-08
tags: [safety]
kind: environmental
governed-by: "0014"
---

Messages between a user and a bot are cloud chats stored on Telegram's servers, not end-to-end encrypted. A key pasted into the chat is visible to Telegram until deleted.
